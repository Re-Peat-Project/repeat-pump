`urzainki2020_peat_tables.csv` was generated by running `peat_hydrol_properties` and `CWTr` from
the blopti code (https://github.com/LukeEcomod/blopti, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2019 Iñaki
Urzainki) for woody peat with von Post 1-8 over the top 0.5 m on sapric peat (Päivänen 1973
retention curves, boreal values), hydraulic conductivity adjust 1. Columns: water table depth
zeta (m, negative below ground), profile water storage (m), transmissivity of the layers below
the water table (m2/day).

Reference: Urzainki, I., Laurén, A., Palviainen, M., Haahti, K., Budiman, A., Basuki, I.,
Netzer, M. and Hökkä, H. (2020). Canal blocking optimization in restoration of drained
peatlands. Biogeosciences 17, 4769-4784. https://doi.org/10.5194/bg-17-4769-2020
